feat(application): implement WAFEngine orchestrating detection + rate limiting

Implements WAFEngine, which combines RateLimiter and DetectionEngine
into a single final decision per request, with configurable dry-run
and fail-open/fail-closed behavior. Closes #7.

- BlockReason is a separate type from domain.Category on purpose: rate
  limiting and internal errors aren't attack categories (SQLi, XSS...),
  they're block reasons of a fundamentally different nature. Reusing
  Category here would mix two concepts the domain layer keeps
  deliberately separate.
- Rate limiting is checked BEFORE rule detection: a map lookup
  (RateLimiter.Allow) is orders of magnitude cheaper than running ~13
  regexes against the full query/body/headers. Rejecting early on
  excess traffic avoids wasting CPU analyzing a request that would be
  discarded anyway — this matters most exactly when the WAF is under a
  DoS-style attack, i.e. when efficiency is most needed.
- Process() wraps both RateLimiter.Allow and DetectionEngine.Evaluate in
  a single defer/recover: neither has an error-returning API today
  (both are designed to never fail in normal operation), so recover()
  here is genuine defense in depth — a future bug (or a malformed rule
  that somehow slips past validation) causing a panic in either must
  never take down the whole proxy process. FailMode (open/closed)
  controls what happens to the request when that recovery kicks in.
- DryRun always wins over FailClosed, even on a recovered internal
  error — validated explicitly with the team. This is consistent with
  what dry-run promises (RF09: safely test new rules in production),
  but it does mean dry-run also masks internal engine failures, not
  just rule false positives. Accepted trade-off, not an oversight.

Tests: 12 tests covering constructor validation, clean requests, rule
violations, dry-run reporting-without-blocking, rate-limit blocking,
rate-limit-before-detection ordering, panic recovery under both fail
modes, dry-run overriding fail-closed on panic, and an explicit
assert.NotPanics regression guard.
